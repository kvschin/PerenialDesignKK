Pocket Prairie Garden Design

Pocket Prairie is built from original work, two open-licensed typefaces, and — where one exists — a photograph somebody else was generous enough to share. This page names all of it.

Design and code

Written and drawn by Kevin Schindler. The plant data is compiled from field experience and standard horticultural references; any error in it is ours, not theirs.

Botanical illustrations

Every plant in the Plant Library is drawn by the app itself, live, four times over — once for each season. They are not stock artwork and not photographs: each one is a procedural recipe for that species' habit, seedhead and seasonal colour, rendered to canvas at the moment you look at it. They are original to Pocket Prairie.

Typefaces
