Privacy Policy

Pocket Prairie Garden Design · Last updated 14 August 2026

Pocket Prairie collects nothing. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and no servers. Your gardens are stored on your own device and are never transmitted anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in, sends no data, and makes no network requests to us or to anyone else. We have no way to identify you, contact you, or see what you design.

What is stored on your device

Everything the app remembers is kept locally, in your browser's or the app's own storage on your device:

  • Your gardens — plants, terrain, structures, plot shape, and any site photo you add.
  • Your preferences — theme, haptics, handedness, plant filters, favourites, and saved plant palettes.

None of it leaves your device unless you deliberately export it. Deleting the app, or clearing its data through your device settings, erases all of it permanently — we hold no copy and cannot restore it for you.

Site photos

If you add a photo of your site to trace over, it is resized and stored on your device like any other part of the garden. It is never uploaded, never analysed, and never seen by us.

Sharing a garden

Sharing is entirely file-based and always started by you. Exporting writes a file you control; you decide whether to send it and to whom. Nothing is published, and there is no server in between.

Third parties

There are none. The app bundles everything it needs — including its typefaces — so it makes no requests to content delivery networks, advertisers, or analytics providers, and works entirely offline.

If you obtained the app from the Apple App Store, Google Play, or Steam, those stores handle your purchase and may collect data under their own privacy policies. We receive only anonymous, aggregate sales figures from them, which cannot identify you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA grant you rights to access, correct, export, and delete your personal data. Since we never collect or hold any, there is nothing for us to disclose or delete. Your garden data is already entirely under your control: the app can export it at any time, and your device settings can erase it.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything — for example optional, anonymous crash reports — this policy will be updated first, the change will be described plainly here, and anything of the sort will be opt-in.
